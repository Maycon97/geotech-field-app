# Geotechnical Safety & Instrumentation Standards
- INA / PZ elevation calculations: `Cota NA = Cota Boca - Profundidade Medida`
- Thresholds checking: Elevacao vs Cota de Atencao, Alerta e Emergencia.
- Any anomaly logged during inspection must include risk classification (Baixo, Médio, Alto) and photographic evidence.
